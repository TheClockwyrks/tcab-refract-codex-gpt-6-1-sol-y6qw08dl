Refract is a quiet puzzle of light and connection. On a dark optical bench, every shape belongs to a channel: amber triangles, sea-green squares, and violet diamonds. A small constellation of nodes waits for you to draw its light.

Press an outlined emitter and trace a beam through every filled lens of its shape, ending at the other emitter. Follow the neighboring nodes, take diagonals, and find a route that leaves room for the other channels. Shared crystals welcome any beam, but every charge must be spent exactly once.

Nothing rushes you. Release a beam to leave it in place, trace backward to undo, or pick up a thread again from either end. A move that does not fit simply stays undrawn. The answer arrives as a glowing picture you made yourself.

Campaign takes you through 24 crafted studies, from a single strand to intricate three-channel compositions. Cascade offers fresh boards without end, climbing through five tiers as your confidence grows. Choose a study, settle in, and draw the light.
